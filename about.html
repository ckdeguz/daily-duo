About Daily Duo

Daily Duo is a free, two-player daily quiz game built for friends, couples, roommates, and anyone who wants to find out how well they really know each other. Every day brings a fresh set of ten questions. The goal isn't just to answer them. It's to guess how the other person will answer.

How the game works

The first player answers ten questions about themselves and, for each one, guesses what their friend would pick. When they're done, they get a unique link to share. The second player opens that link, answers the same ten questions, and guesses the first player's answers in return. As soon as both players finish, the results screen reveals two scores side by side, showing how well each person predicted the other.

Everyone playing on the same day gets the same set of questions, so you can compare your duo's score against how other pairs did, then come back tomorrow for a brand-new round.

Play as a guest or create an account

You can jump in and play right away as a guest, with no sign-up required. If you'd like to keep your history, you can sign in with a Google account. Signing in unlocks a few extra features while keeping the quick, casual feel of the game intact.

Friends, leaderboards, and your dashboard

Signed-in players can add friends by username or invite link, then track an ongoing rivalry over time. Each pair of friends has its own leaderboard with all-time and monthly best scores, so you can see who's been reading the other's mind most accurately. Your personal dashboard rounds it out with your full game history, overall stats, and a breakdown of how you've done against each friend.

Free and ad-supported

Daily Duo is completely free to play. We keep it that way with unobtrusive advertising provided through Google AdSense. If you'd like to know how we handle your data and advertising, see our Privacy Policy.

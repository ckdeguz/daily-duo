Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Daily Duo ("we", "us", "the app") is a free two-player daily quiz game. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Daily Duo you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

You can play Daily Duo as a guest without an account. If you choose to sign in, or as you play, we collect:

  • Google account information. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo from Google to create your account. Your name, profile photo, and username are visible to other players so they can find and recognize you. Your email address is held by Google's Firebase Authentication to sign you in; it is not published in your public profile and is not shown to other players.
  • Gameplay data. The answers and guesses you submit, your scores, the friends you connect with, and your leaderboard results. This is needed to run the game and show results to you and the people you play with.
  • A guest identifier. If you play without an account, we store a random identifier in your browser's local storage to keep your in-progress and finished games linked to you on your device.
  • Theme and preference data stored locally in your browser.

How we use your information

We use the information above only to operate Daily Duo: to run the quiz, match and score answers between you and the person you play with, maintain friends and leaderboards, and show your personal dashboard. We do not sell your personal information.

How your data is stored

Daily Duo uses Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Firebase Hosting) to provide sign-in and to store accounts and game data. Your data is processed and stored on Google's infrastructure subject to Google's security practices.

Cookies and local storage

Daily Duo is free to play and does not show ads. We do not use advertising cookies and we do not allow third-party advertising networks to track you on this site.

  • If you sign in with Google, Firebase Authentication sets cookies and browser storage needed to keep you signed in.
  • We use your browser's local storage to remember your theme preference and, if you play as a guest, a random identifier that lets us attach a finished game to your account if you sign in later. This identifier is not linked to your real-world identity.
  • For more information on how Google handles data for the Firebase services we use, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Your choices

You can play as a guest without signing in. You can clear cookies and local storage at any time through your browser settings. To request deletion of your account and associated data, contact us at the email below.

Children's privacy

Daily Duo is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email us at dailyduoapp@gmail.com.

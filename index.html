Daily Duo

A free two-player daily quiz game. Answer ten questions, guess your friend's answers, share a link, and compare scores.

Every day brings ten fresh questions. You answer them about yourself and guess how your friend will answer, then share a link so they can do the same. When you both finish, you'll see how well each of you predicted the other. Sign in with Google to add friends, climb per-pair leaderboards, and track your stats on a personal dashboard — or play instantly as a guest.
